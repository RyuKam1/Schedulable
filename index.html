How to Use Schedulable

Your personal shift & break planner, in six quick steps

1 Copy your opening message

Click Opening message in the navbar to copy it and paste into your chats. Use the pencil to change the text.

2 Set your shift

In Shift Settings, pick Full-Time or Part-Time and enter your start & end times. Change them freely anytime, and add Overtime if you have it.

3 Preview your day

The live clock dial and the timeline table show every work block and break at a glance. Hover any block for its details.

4 Fit breaks your way

Drag breaks on the clock, use the sliders, or type exact times. Anything that breaks a rule turns red, and the navbar badge tells you why.

5 Get recommended breaks

Tap a One-Click Preset for a ready-made, rule-friendly layout, or hit Auto-Fix Breaks to repair any schedule instantly.

6 Take it anywhere

Your schedule saves automatically. Use Port to copy it to another browser or device.

Leave this tab open in the background

  • • The clock shows the exact current time in real time, and the tab title & icon show live countdowns to your next break or shift end.
  • • Turn Alerts ON in the navbar to get a desktop notification, a chime, and an on-screen banner 5 minutes before breaks, shift start, and shift end.
  • • Press Test to preview what an alert looks and sounds like.
